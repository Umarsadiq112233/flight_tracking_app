  • Flight details
    Vertical speed See how quickly an aircraft is ascending or descending.
    Squawk code A four-digit code assigned to a flight by air traffic control to match the flight to its radar screens.
    GPS altitude (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) The aircraft's altitude reported by GPS, based on an ellipsoid of the entire earth and not necessarily indicative of altitude above ground.
    True airspeed (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) True airspeed is the airspeed of an aircraft corrected for position and instrument error and taking pressure and temperature into account.
    Wind (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) See if a flight is flying with the wind or against it. View the wind speed and direction reported directly from the aircraft.
    Indicated airspeed (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) Indicated airspeed is the speed of the aircraft through the air taken directly from airspeed indicator without any corrections for temperature or pressure.
    Temperature (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) See the outside air temperature reported directly from the aircraft.
    FIR/UIR (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) See which air traffic controllers are currently responsible for a flight thanks to worldwide Flight Information Region (FIR) and Upper Information Region (UIR) data.
    Mach (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) A Mach number is the ratio of true airspeed to the speed of sound at the altitude of a given flight.

  • Weather layers
    Current weather Sunny and warm in Madrid, raining and cold in San Francisco. See the current weather, including temperature and sky conditions, for over 3,000 airports worldwide overlaid on our live map.
    Cloud (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) Global IR Satellite provides worldwide cloud cover displayed on the map, refreshed every 60 minutes.
    Total precipitation (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) An overview of current global precipitation overlaid on our live map. The total precipitation layer is refreshed 12 times a day.
    Intense precipitation (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) See the weather flights are avoiding overlaid on our live map. Global Radar provides worldwide areas of intense precipitation so you'll know how much of an impact it could have on flights. The intense precipitation layer is refreshed every 30 minutes.
    Australian radar (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) Australian radar is a composite image of precipitation throughout Australia at 2 km resolution.
    North American radar (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) High-resolution North American Radar is a mosaic image of radars covering the continental US, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and the southern portion of Canada. High-resolution North American Radar combines 1 km resolution with frequent updates for the best weather detail and timeliness.
  • Map layers
    ATC boundaries (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) See which air traffic controllers are currently responsible for a flight with worldwide Flight Information Region (FIR) and Upper Information Region (UIR) boundaries overlaid on map. The current ATC area for an individual flight is also shown in the flight information box for that flight.
    Oceanic tracks (GOLD-ONLY FEATURE) Over areas like the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, flights follow organized tracks to stay safely separated when outside contact with air traffic control. The tracks are updated twice daily to take wind and weather into account. View these tracks on our live map and see how flights follow them to safely navigate over open ocean.
